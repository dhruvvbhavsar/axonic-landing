# Clinical Data Analyst - Healthcare Innovation

Join our Clinical Affairs team as a Clinical Data Analyst and play a crucial role in transforming healthcare through data-driven insights. Help us analyze clinical data to improve patient outcomes and support evidence-based medical decisions.

## About the Role

As a Clinical Data Analyst at Axonic Health, you'll be responsible for analyzing complex clinical datasets to generate insights that drive healthcare innovation. You'll work with cutting-edge healthcare data from around the world, contributing to research that improves patient care globally.

## What You'll Do

### Data Analysis & Modeling
- Analyze clinical trial data, electronic health records, and real-world evidence
- Develop and validate statistical models for predicting health outcomes
- Perform survival analysis, regression modeling, and machine learning applications
- Conduct comparative effectiveness research and health economics studies

### Research & Insights
- Design and execute analytical studies to answer clinical questions
- Identify trends and patterns in large healthcare datasets
- Generate evidence to support clinical decision-making and policy development
- Contribute to peer-reviewed publications and conference presentations

### Quality & Compliance
- Ensure data integrity and quality throughout the analysis process
- Maintain compliance with regulatory standards (GCP, GDPR, FDA guidelines)
- Document analytical methods and validate results
- Support regulatory submissions and health technology assessments

### Collaboration & Communication
- Work closely with clinical researchers, biostatisticians, and medical affairs teams
- Present findings to internal stakeholders and external partners
- Translate complex analytical results into actionable insights
- Support clinical teams in study design and protocol development

## Required Qualifications

- **Education**: Master's degree in Biostatistics, Epidemiology, Public Health, or related quantitative field
- **Experience**: 2-4 years in clinical data analysis, preferably in healthcare or pharmaceutical industry
- **Technical Skills**: Proficiency in R, SAS, Python, or similar statistical software
- **Clinical Knowledge**: Understanding of clinical trial methodology and healthcare data structures
- **Regulatory Awareness**: Familiarity with clinical research regulations and data privacy requirements

## Preferred Qualifications

- PhD in relevant field
- Experience with machine learning and AI applications in healthcare
- Knowledge of health economics and outcomes research (HEOR)
- Familiarity with healthcare databases (CPRD, THIN, claims databases)
- Experience with cloud-based analytics platforms

## Our Data Environment

- **Clinical Trials**: Phase I-IV trial data from global studies
- **Real-World Data**: Electronic health records, claims data, patient registries
- **Global Health Data**: Population health data from multiple countries
- **Advanced Analytics**: Machine learning, AI, and predictive modeling tools

## Career Development

- **Specialization**: Develop expertise in specific therapeutic areas or analytical methods
- **Leadership**: Opportunities to lead analytical projects and mentor junior analysts
- **Research**: Contribute to high-impact research publications and presentations
- **Global Impact**: Work on projects that influence healthcare policy and practice worldwide

## Why Join Our Team?

- **Meaningful Work**: Your analysis directly impacts patient care and health outcomes
- **Innovation**: Work with the latest analytical methods and healthcare technologies
- **Global Reach**: Analyze data from healthcare systems around the world
- **Growth**: Continuous learning opportunities in a rapidly evolving field
- **Collaboration**: Work with world-class clinical researchers and data scientists

Ready to make a difference in global healthcare through data analysis? Apply now and join our mission to improve health outcomes worldwide!
