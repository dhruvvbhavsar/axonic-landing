# Product Manager - Healthcare Solutions

Join Axonic Health as a Product Manager and shape the future of healthcare technology. Lead the development of products that connect healthcare providers and patients worldwide, making quality healthcare accessible to everyone.

## About This Opportunity

As a Product Manager at Axonic Health, you'll be responsible for driving the product strategy and execution for our healthcare technology platform. You'll work at the intersection of technology, healthcare, and user experience to create solutions that truly make a difference in people's lives.

## Key Responsibilities

### Product Strategy & Vision
- Define and communicate product vision aligned with company goals
- Develop comprehensive product roadmaps based on market research and customer feedback
- Identify new market opportunities and product expansion areas

### Cross-functional Leadership
- Collaborate with engineering teams to deliver high-quality products on time
- Work with design teams to create intuitive user experiences
- Partner with clinical experts to ensure medical accuracy and compliance

### Customer-Centric Development
- Conduct user research and gather feedback from healthcare professionals
- Analyze user behavior and product metrics to drive improvements
- Represent the voice of the customer in all product decisions

### Market Analysis
- Monitor healthcare technology trends and competitive landscape
- Identify regulatory requirements and ensure product compliance
- Evaluate market opportunities and business impact

## What We're Looking For

- **Healthcare Passion**: Genuine interest in improving healthcare outcomes through technology
- **Strategic Thinking**: Ability to see the big picture while managing detailed execution
- **Data-Driven**: Strong analytical skills with experience in product metrics and KPIs
- **Communication**: Excellent written and verbal communication skills
- **Collaboration**: Proven ability to work effectively with cross-functional teams
- **Adaptability**: Comfortable working in a fast-paced, evolving environment

## Our Product Ecosystem

- **Global OPD Platform**: Telemedicine solutions connecting patients and doctors worldwide
- **Healthcare Data Integration**: Interoperable systems for seamless health information exchange
- **AI-Powered Diagnostics**: Machine learning tools for improved clinical decision-making
- **Mobile Health Applications**: Patient-facing apps for health monitoring and engagement

## Career Growth

- **Leadership Development**: Opportunities to lead larger product initiatives
- **Healthcare Expertise**: Deep dive into healthcare technology and regulations
- **Global Impact**: Work on products used by healthcare systems worldwide
- **Innovation**: Be part of cutting-edge healthcare technology development

## Application Process

1. **Application Review**: Initial screening of resume and cover letter
2. **Product Case Study**: Present a product strategy for a healthcare scenario
3. **Team Interviews**: Meet with engineering, design, and clinical teams
4. **Executive Interview**: Discussion with senior leadership about vision and fit
