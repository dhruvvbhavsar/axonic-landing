## Requirements

- Bachelor’s degree in Business, Healthcare Management, Pharmacy, or a related field (MBA preferred)  
- 3–5 years of proven experience in business development or healthcare sales, with at least 1–2 years in a managerial capacity  
- Strong network in the Mumbai healthcare ecosystem is a plus  
- Exceptional communication, leadership, and negotiation skills  
- Proficiency in CRM tools and MS Office Suite  
- Ability to work independently with a strong sense of accountability  

## Responsibilities

### 1. Strategic Partnerships & Expansion

- Lead the acquisition and onboarding of specialist doctors and healthcare institutions  
- Establish strategic partnerships with nursing homes and small hospitals to set up Axoncare outreach clinics  
- Own and drive partnership negotiations and formalization processes  

### 2. Team Leadership

- Manage and mentor a team of Business Development Executives to ensure high performance and target achievement  
- Set clear goals, provide ongoing feedback, and facilitate training and development initiatives  

### 3. Market Intelligence & Growth Planning

- Conduct in-depth market research to identify expansion opportunities, emerging trends, and competitive insights  
- Develop and execute territory expansion strategies aligned with company objectives  

### 4. Stakeholder Engagement

- Build and maintain strong relationships with key healthcare stakeholders and decision-makers  
- Serve as the escalation point for complex client needs and ensure smooth issue resolution  

### 5. Performance Monitoring & Reporting

- Define KPIs and track performance metrics across onboarding, partnerships, and revenue goals  
- Provide regular reports and actionable insights to senior leadership
