# Marketing Specialist - Healthcare Technology

Join Axonic Health as a Marketing Specialist and help us spread the word about our revolutionary healthcare technology solutions. Play a key role in building our brand and connecting with healthcare professionals and patients around the world.

## About This Role

As a Marketing Specialist at Axonic Health, you'll be responsible for developing and executing marketing campaigns that showcase our innovative healthcare solutions. You'll work in a fast-paced environment where your creativity and strategic thinking will directly impact our growth and mission to make healthcare accessible globally.

## What You'll Do

### Digital Marketing Campaigns
- Plan and execute multi-channel digital marketing campaigns
- Develop email marketing sequences for different audience segments
- Create and optimize landing pages for lead generation
- Manage paid advertising campaigns across Google, LinkedIn, and healthcare publications

### Content Creation & Management
- Write compelling blog posts, case studies, and whitepapers
- Create engaging social media content for LinkedIn, Twitter, and healthcare forums
- Develop marketing collateral including brochures, presentations, and infographics
- Collaborate with clinical experts to create educational content

### Analytics & Optimization
- Track and analyze marketing performance metrics and KPIs
- Conduct A/B tests to optimize campaign performance
- Generate regular reports on marketing ROI and lead quality
- Use data insights to refine marketing strategies and tactics

### Brand & Community Building
- Maintain consistent brand voice across all marketing channels
- Engage with healthcare communities and thought leaders
- Support PR initiatives and media relations
- Represent the company at virtual and in-person healthcare events

## What We're Looking For

### Marketing Expertise
- **Digital Marketing**: Strong understanding of digital marketing channels and best practices
- **Content Marketing**: Proven ability to create engaging, educational content
- **Analytics**: Experience with Google Analytics, marketing automation platforms, and CRM systems
- **Healthcare Knowledge**: Interest in healthcare technology and understanding of healthcare audiences

### Skills & Qualities
- **Creative Thinking**: Ability to develop innovative marketing campaigns and content ideas
- **Data-Driven**: Comfortable using data to make decisions and optimize performance
- **Communication**: Excellent writing skills and ability to communicate complex topics simply
- **Collaboration**: Experience working with cross-functional teams including sales and product

### Technical Proficiency
- Marketing automation platforms (HubSpot, Marketo, Pardot)
- Social media management tools (Hootsuite, Buffer, Sprout Social)
- Design tools (Canva, Adobe Creative Suite basics)
- CRM systems and lead management

## Our Marketing Environment

### Target Audiences
- **Healthcare Providers**: Doctors, nurses, hospital administrators
- **Healthcare Systems**: Large health networks and integrated delivery systems
- **Government Health Agencies**: Public health organizations and policy makers
- **Patients**: Individuals seeking accessible healthcare solutions

### Marketing Channels
- **Digital**: Website, SEO, paid search, social media, email marketing
- **Content**: Blog, whitepapers, case studies, webinars, podcasts
- **Events**: Healthcare conferences, trade shows, virtual events
- **Partnerships**: Co-marketing with healthcare organizations and technology partners

## Growth Opportunities

- **Specialization**: Develop expertise in healthcare marketing or specific therapeutic areas
- **Leadership**: Opportunities to lead marketing projects and mentor junior team members
- **Global Impact**: Work on campaigns that reach healthcare systems worldwide
- **Innovation**: Be part of marketing cutting-edge healthcare technology solutions

## Why Join Our Marketing Team?

- **Mission-Driven**: Your marketing efforts directly support global healthcare accessibility
- **Innovation**: Market groundbreaking healthcare technology solutions
- **Growth**: Rapid career advancement opportunities in a fast-growing company
- **Impact**: See the direct results of your marketing efforts on business growth
