# UX Designer - Healthcare Applications

Join Axonic Health as a UX Designer and create user experiences that make healthcare more accessible, intuitive, and effective for millions of users worldwide. Design solutions that bridge the gap between complex healthcare systems and the people who need care.

## About the Role

As a UX Designer at Axonic Health, you'll be responsible for designing user experiences that serve diverse healthcare stakeholders - from patients seeking care to doctors providing treatment to administrators managing health systems. Your designs will directly impact how people access and receive healthcare globally.

## What You'll Design

### Patient-Facing Applications
- Telemedicine platforms for virtual consultations
- Health monitoring and wellness tracking applications
- Patient portals for accessing medical records and test results
- Appointment scheduling and care coordination tools

### Healthcare Provider Tools
- Clinical decision support systems
- Electronic health record interfaces
- Diagnostic and treatment planning applications
- Practice management and workflow optimization tools

### Administrative Systems
- Healthcare analytics and reporting dashboards
- Population health management interfaces
- Insurance and billing management systems
- Regulatory compliance and audit tools

## Key Responsibilities

### User Research & Analysis
- Conduct user interviews with patients, healthcare providers, and administrators
- Perform usability testing and analyze user behavior data
- Create user personas, journey maps, and service blueprints
- Identify pain points and opportunities in healthcare workflows

### Design & Prototyping
- Create wireframes, mockups, and interactive prototypes
- Design responsive interfaces for web and mobile platforms
- Develop and maintain design systems and component libraries
- Ensure designs meet accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1 AA)

### Collaboration & Communication
- Work closely with product managers, engineers, and clinical experts
- Present design concepts and rationale to stakeholders
- Participate in design reviews and iterate based on feedback
- Advocate for user needs in product planning discussions

### Healthcare-Specific Considerations
- Design for diverse user groups with varying technical literacy
- Ensure compliance with healthcare regulations (HIPAA, GDPR)
- Consider clinical workflows and medical terminology
- Design for high-stress, time-critical healthcare environments

## What We're Looking For

### Design Skills
- **User-Centered Design**: Strong foundation in UX principles and methodologies
- **Visual Design**: Excellent visual design skills with attention to detail
- **Interaction Design**: Experience designing complex, multi-step user flows
- **Prototyping**: Proficiency in creating interactive prototypes for testing

### Healthcare Understanding
- **Domain Knowledge**: Interest in healthcare systems and medical workflows
- **Empathy**: Understanding of patient needs and healthcare provider challenges
- **Compliance Awareness**: Familiarity with healthcare regulations and accessibility standards
- **Research Skills**: Experience conducting research in sensitive or regulated environments

### Technical Collaboration
- **Cross-functional Work**: Experience collaborating with engineers and product teams
- **Design Systems**: Knowledge of creating and maintaining scalable design systems
- **Agile Process**: Comfortable working in agile development environments
- **Data-Driven**: Ability to use analytics and user feedback to inform design decisions

## Our Design Philosophy

- **Human-Centered**: Every design decision starts with understanding user needs
- **Inclusive**: We design for accessibility and diverse user populations
- **Evidence-Based**: We validate designs through research and testing
- **Systematic**: We build scalable design systems that ensure consistency
- **Collaborative**: We work closely with all stakeholders to create the best solutions

## Career Growth Opportunities

- **Specialization**: Develop expertise in specific areas like clinical UX or patient experience
- **Leadership**: Opportunities to lead design projects and mentor junior designers
- **Research**: Contribute to UX research publications and healthcare design best practices
- **Impact**: See your designs used by healthcare systems serving millions of patients
