# Senior Software Engineer - Healthcare Technology

Are you passionate about using technology to revolutionize healthcare? Join Axonic Health as a Senior Software Engineer and help us build the future of global healthcare delivery.

## About the Role

As a Senior Software Engineer at Axonic Health, you'll be at the forefront of developing innovative healthcare solutions that connect patients, doctors, and healthcare systems worldwide. You'll work on challenging problems that directly impact patient care and healthcare accessibility.

## What You'll Do

- **Build Scalable Solutions**: Develop robust, scalable applications that can handle millions of users across different healthcare systems
- **Healthcare Innovation**: Work on cutting-edge features like AI-powered diagnostics, telemedicine platforms, and global health data interoperability
- **Cross-functional Collaboration**: Partner with product managers, designers, and healthcare professionals to create user-centric solutions
- **Technical Leadership**: Guide architectural decisions and mentor team members
- **Quality Assurance**: Ensure our applications meet the highest standards of security, performance, and reliability

## Our Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: React, TypeScript, Next.js, Tailwind CSS
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express, Python, FastAPI
- **Database**: PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis
- **Cloud**: AWS, Docker, Kubernetes
- **Healthcare**: HL7 FHIR, DICOM, healthcare APIs

## Why Join Axonic Health?

- **Mission-Driven**: Make a real impact on global healthcare accessibility
- **Growth Opportunities**: Rapid career advancement in a fast-growing company
- **Innovation**: Work with the latest technologies and healthcare standards
- **Global Impact**: Your work will reach patients and healthcare providers worldwide
- **Team Culture**: Collaborative, supportive, and diverse team environment

## Application Process

1. **Initial Screening**: Resume review and brief phone call
2. **Technical Interview**: Coding challenge and system design discussion
3. **Team Interview**: Meet with potential teammates and discuss collaboration
4. **Final Interview**: Culture fit and leadership discussion with senior team

Ready to revolutionize healthcare technology? We'd love to hear from you!
